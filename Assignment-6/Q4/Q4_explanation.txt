Question 4 explanation

syncdeadlock creates five real xv6 children. Each worker repeatedly needs two of
three resource types: Printer, Scanner, and Disk. The semaphore capacities are
Printer=2, Scanner=1, and Disk=2, so the semaphore counts represent the
limited shared resource instances.

All workers acquire their two resources in the global order Printer < Scanner <
Disk. They print request, grant, work, release, and completion markers for
three cycles. The ordered acquisition rule prevents circular wait, while the
kernel-backed semaphores ensure that no resource count becomes negative or
exceeds its configured capacity. Shared memory records each worker's completed
cycle count so the parent can verify that all five workers finish.
